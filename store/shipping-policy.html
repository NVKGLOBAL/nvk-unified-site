Insured Global Logistics & Shipping Policy

All hardware artifacts ordered through NVK Global are packaged in electrostatic-safe, reinforced protective containers and dispatched within 24 hours of payment verification.

Transit Insurance: 100% of shipments carry comprehensive transit insurance covering loss, transit damage, or logistical delay until delivery confirmation.

Carriers: We partner exclusively with DHL Express, FedEx Priority, and certified white-glove couriers. Tracking details are dispatched to the checkout email.

Delivery timelines vary between 2–5 business days depending on destination. Local customs and import duties are covered where specified.
