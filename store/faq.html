FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers here use only what the product catalog and the published policies actually state.

How do I buy a product?

Click Buy Now on any product page. You go directly to secure Stripe checkout for that product. There is no account to create and no shopping cart.

What payment methods are accepted?

Checkout is handled securely by Stripe. Apple Pay, Google Pay and major cards are accepted. All prices are listed in USD.

How fast will my order ship?

Orders are dispatched within 24 hours of payment verification. Delivery takes 2–5 business days depending on destination.

Is shipping insured?

Yes. 100% of shipments carry comprehensive transit insurance covering loss, transit damage, or logistical delay until delivery confirmation.

Who delivers my order?

NVK Global partners exclusively with DHL Express, FedEx Priority, and certified white-glove couriers.

Is shipping free?

The shipping policy does not state that shipping is free, so we do not claim it here. See the shipping policy for what is stated.

What is the returns policy?

If a product does not meet your expectations, return it in original packaging within 30 days for a full refund with zero restocking fees.

What warranty do products carry?

Every product is backed by a direct 24-month hardware replacement warranty against manufacturing defects, internal component failure, and electronic faults.

How do I start a warranty claim or replacement?

Contact our concierge at support@nvk.global.

Is there an NVK token I can buy?

No. There is no token for sale and no public sale. Any future NVK token direction is proposed utility only.

How do I contact NVK Global?

By email only: support@nvk.global. No phone number or postal address is published.

Do I need an account to buy?

No. Checkout is direct through Stripe — no account, no shopping cart.
