Our Story

Headphone Heroes

One story universe across music, manga, and video. The sound, the story, and the hardware are the same world — told in every medium NVK Global makes.

Canon

Book 1: Awakening the Frequency

Headphone Heroes — Book 1: Awakening the Frequency

  • A 79-page manuscript. Max Carter. Fifty alien headphones carrying the word “Listen”.
  • Episode 01: The Signal — the manga where it begins. The TIME MACHINE appears in-story, wrist-worn, its display reading “IDENTITY CONFIRMED / WELCOME, ARCHITECT”.
  • Characters: The Architect, Photon-9, Sophia-9.

Where Book 1 ends

The hero found the waveform — the sound the Silence absorbed from a hundred worlds. The broken waveform opened a door. The door is two-way: opening it further lets the Silence into his world, not trapped sounds out.

He sealed the door by stepping through it himself, closing it from the Silence’s side. He is trapped there.

The Trade. The Silence offered it: it releases every absorbed world if he stays and becomes its new voice. His answer is still open. He has not accepted the Trade.

Proposed Canon

Book 2: The Exodus

He does not wait for rescue, and he does not accept the Trade — not yet. There is a third way out: the Exodus.

Proposed canon — awaiting NvK’s approval. Book 2’s direction was decided by NvK on 2026-11-14. Every detail below is proposed until he approves or corrects it. Nothing here promotes itself to canon.

The Silence’s side is not one place. It is the absorbed worlds themselves, folded together — every world the Silence ever swallowed, still humming underneath, waiting to be heard again. To get home, he must cross them: one world per chapter, one stolen sound per chapter. Every sound he frees weakens the Silence’s hold and lights one more step of the way back to the door.

He carries one thing from his world: the first note — the first note he ever kept. It hums louder near each world’s stolen sound. It is his compass.

  • The Trade stays unanswered. The Silence offers it again at every crossing — a better version each time. The hero never accepts it in Book 2 unless NvK says so.
  • The Silence sings now. It learned to sing. It is no longer a mute monster — it talks, it taunts, it bargains, it sings back.
  • Each crossing marks him. Every world he crosses leaves a visible mark — a color, a scar, a note on his suit, a change in his headphones. The hero must look different in Chapter 3 than in Chapter 1.
  • The door stays open behind him. Every sound he frees, every step toward home, is also a step the Silence could follow back through. The return has a cost.

Who crosses with him

The Hero

Max Carter’s line — the Headphone Hero. Trapped on the Silence’s side, crossing home. Black suit, glowing cyan headphones, N-V-K on his back, round TIME MACHINE on his wrist. He changes as he crosses.

The Silence

Antagonist and chorus. It ate every sound in a hundred worlds — and now it sings. It offers the Trade at every crossing.

Sophia-9

She holds the door from the Nexus side. She can hear him — faintly, through the first note — and she answers. She is the voice from home.

Photon-9

The runner. He carries the freed sounds back through the door, one at a time, faster than the Silence can re-swallow them.

The Architect

He built the door’s frame, and he knows what it cost. He appears when the crossing needs a way through that doesn’t exist yet.

The Hatchlings

Small waveform creatures, freed one world at a time. They follow the hero — an audience that grows with every chapter.
